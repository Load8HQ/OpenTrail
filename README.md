#OpenTrail
OpenTrail is a human-scale AI platform that helps local authorities and destination managers anticipate visitor demand, understand movement patterns, and make better operational decisions.

#Why OpenTrail?
Public spaces, parks, and visitor destinations often struggle to understand changing visitor demand. OpenTrail combines operational data, forecasting, and analytics to support better planning while keeping people in control of decisions.

#Core Principles
Small
Purpose-built forecasting and analytics systems designed for specific operational challenges.

#Intentional
Transparent use of data with human oversight and accountability.

#Tailored
Built specifically for local authorities, destination managers, and public spaces.

To demonstrate how human-scale AI can support communities, public services, and shared public spaces through transparent and accountable decision support systems.
