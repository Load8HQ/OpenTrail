# OpenTrail

OpenTrail is a human-scale AI platform designed to help local authorities, parks, and visitor destinations anticipate demand, understand movement patterns, and make better operational decisions.

## Why OpenTrail?

Many public spaces struggle to predict visitor demand, manage congestion, allocate resources, and protect natural environments.

OpenTrail combines forecasting, operational intelligence, and transparent analytics to help organizations make better decisions while keeping humans in control.

## Human-Scale AI Principles

### Small
Purpose-built forecasting and analytics models designed for specific operational problems.

### Intentional
Human oversight, transparent decision-making, and privacy-conscious data practices.

### Tailored
Built specifically for parks, visitor destinations, local authorities, and community spaces.

## Potential Use Cases

- Visitor demand forecasting
- Congestion prediction
- Resource allocation
- Sustainable destination management
- Environmental stewardship
- Public sector operational intelligence

## Project Status

Early-stage prototype.

## Vision

To help communities manage shared spaces through transparent, accountable, and human-centered AI.
